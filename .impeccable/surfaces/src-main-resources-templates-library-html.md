# ManaCheck workspace redesign

Scope: library, editor, revision comparison, and shared shell. Mode: Operate.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Deck maintenance leads every screen. The user pinned Moxfield's compact working interface and a San Diego sunset palette; remove the incumbent marketing hero and repeated slogans.

OWN-WORLD: Pacific navy shell, blue slate work surfaces, coral primary actions, apricot selection and chart marks, readable off-white text. One system sans, native controls, full card thumbnails, aligned rows. No ornamental cards or display serif.

STORY: Find a saved deck, change its list, save, inspect findings, review a suggestion, compare revisions. Short labels and progressive disclosure keep the workflow readable.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact horizontal header; library heading and deck search above illustrated rows; an inline new-deck form at the right. The editor puts commander, dense card list, and analysis into three working columns. The small sun-over-water mark is the signature move; actual card imagery anchors the deck rows.

FORM: User-pinned category convention (Moxfield), overriding assigned candidate 7. Seed 62f4c80e. Code-led, explicitly confirmed by user. Grounded directions considered: deck-library table, card binder index, tournament registration sheet, surf forecast, marina manifest, concert setlist, botanical specimen index. User commitment settles the choice. Challengers are declined under the pinned brief; retain only their disciplines: column alignment, artwork clarity, state contrast, and visible change history.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Constraints: retain existing IDs, workflows, accessible feedback, image fallback, and source/AI/rules caveats. No backend rule changes. Desktop and mobile captured together in one inspection round; one correction/confirmation round maximum.
